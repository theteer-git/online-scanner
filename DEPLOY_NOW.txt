ONLINE SCANNER — CLOUDFLARE PAGES DIRECT UPLOAD

1. Sign in to Cloudflare.
2. Open Workers & Pages.
3. Select Create application.
4. Choose Pages / Drag and drop your files.
5. Use a hard-to-guess project name, for example:
   scanner-lab-7x4m2q
6. Upload THIS ZIP file, or upload the extracted folder.
7. Select Deploy site.
8. Open the generated:
   https://<project-name>.pages.dev
9. Test from both desktop and mobile.
10. Share the URL only with intended testers.

This is link-only preview access, not authentication.
Anyone with the exact URL can use the scanner.

Documents are processed locally in the browser and are not uploaded by this app.
